## The idea
Most booking plugins are heavy and full of features small businesses never use. Slotly does one job: let customers pick a time slot and remind them before they arrive.

## What I built
- A booking block that can be placed on any page
- A React-based admin calendar for viewing and moving bookings
- Working hours, breaks and holidays per staff member
- Email reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before each appointment (WP-Cron)
- Custom REST API endpoints, with nonces and capability checks

## What I learned
Building this taught me to design plugin settings that stay simple, and to write database tables that hold up as bookings grow.
