## The idea
Shri Sai Vidhya Niketan wanted its own monthly school newspaper in Hindi, **"विद्या वाणी"**, that staff could put together without design software, read on the website, and print.

## What I built
- **A visual A4 page editor** in the admin panel: drag items to move them, drag the edges to resize, and add page breaks
- **A real newspaper layout:** a front-page nameplate, a lead story and columns, and a one-third sidebar with the index, thought for the day, did-you-know, events and a puzzle
- **Page furniture handled automatically:** an automatic table of contents, page numbers, a footer on every page, credits on every item, and a back page with the school's address, social links and a QR code
- **Print to PDF** straight from the browser, using Paged.js to lay out the pages
- **A web reader and an archive** of past issues on the website

## How it evolved
It started as a magazine called "साई दर्पण". After seeing it in use, we switched to a newspaper layout, which fits more content per page and feels more like a real school paper.

## Why it's interesting
Getting reliable print layout out of a web browser is hard. Combining Paged.js with a drag-and-resize editor gives the school a publishing tool built specifically for them.
