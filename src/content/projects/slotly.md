## The idea
Small service businesses lose money to no-shows and spend hours on the phone managing appointments. Slotly lets customers book a free slot themselves and reminds them before they arrive.

## What I built
- A booking page customers can open from a link or a QR code
- An admin calendar for viewing, moving and cancelling bookings
- Working hours, breaks and holidays for each staff member
- Email and SMS reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before each appointment
- A secure REST API, so the same backend can power a mobile app later

## What I learned
Designing around time zones, double-booking and cancellations taught me a lot about building reliable scheduling logic.
