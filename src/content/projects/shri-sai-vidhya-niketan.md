## The client
Shri Sai Vidhya Niketan is a school that was managing admissions, attendance, fees and parent communication on paper registers, spreadsheets and phone calls. They wanted one system to run the school and a better way to stay in touch with parents.

## What I built

### 1. School ERP (for the office and teachers)
- Student records and admissions, from enquiry to enrolment
- Daily attendance by class, with reports for teachers and management
- Fee management: fee structures, receipts, dues and payment history
- Exams and report cards
- Timetables, staff records and notices
- Role-based access, so the principal, office staff and teachers each see only what they need

### 2. School website (for parents and the public)
- Information about the school, academics and facilities
- An online admission enquiry form that goes straight into the ERP
- News, events and a photo gallery the school updates itself

### 3. Mobile app (for parents)
- Attendance, homework and notices for each child
- Fee dues and payment history
- Instant notifications for announcements and holidays

## The result
The school now runs admissions, attendance, fees and parent communication from one place, and parents get updates on their phones instead of waiting for a call or a note in the diary.
