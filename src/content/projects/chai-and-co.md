## The idea
Small tea brands sell mostly on Instagram and WhatsApp, which makes repeat orders messy. Chai & Co. is a concept store that turns those followers into subscribers.

## What I built
- A custom block theme with a warm, packaging-inspired design system in `theme.json`
- Monthly tea subscriptions with pause and skip options
- A gift-box builder where shoppers pick three teas and add a note
- Razorpay checkout with UPI, plus COD limited by pin code
- Order updates sent to WhatsApp through a small custom plugin

## Why it matters for a real client
Every part of this store can be edited from the block editor. The owner can launch a new tea, change prices or run a festival sale without calling a developer.
